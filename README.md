# Customer Churn Risk Analysis using Business Intelligence (Power BI)
This project focuses on identifying and analyzing customer churn risk using a Business Intelligence approach. The analysis is performed using Power BI, where customer data is cleaned, transformed, and visualized to uncover patterns and trends.

A rule-based churn risk model is developed to classify customers into high, medium, and low-risk segments based on factors such as tenure and monthly charges. The project also highlights revenue impact and provides actionable insights to improve customer retention.
## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze customer behavior and churn patterns
- Identify key factors influencing churn
- Develop churn risk segmentation (High, Medium, Low)
- Build interactive dashboards using Power BI
- Evaluate revenue impact of customer churn
- Provide business insights for decision-making
  ## 🛠 Tools & Technologies

- Power BI (Data Visualization & Dashboard)
- Microsoft Excel (Data Cleaning)
- DAX (Data Modeling & Calculations)
  ## 📂 Dataset

- Source: Kaggle (Telco Customer Churn Dataset)
- Contains customer demographics, service usage, billing, and churn status
  ## 🚀 Key Features

- Churn Rate Analysis
- Customer Segmentation (Tenure, Charges, Contract)
- Rule-Based Churn Risk Identification
- Revenue Loss Analysis
- Interactive Dashboard with Drill-through Feature
  ## 🔍 Key Insights

- Customers with month-to-month contracts have higher churn rates
- High monthly charges increase churn risk
- Customers with low tenure are more likely to churn
- High-risk customers contribute significantly to revenue loss
  ## 💼 Business Impact

- Helps identify high-risk customers early
- Supports data-driven decision-making
- Improves customer retention strategies
- Reduces revenue loss
